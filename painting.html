01. Birch Forest

mom_01_birch_forest_original_work.jpg

This original and expressive landscape painting by Lin Wang captures a dense birch forest alive with late-season color. White trunks rise and twist through a layered field of golden leaves, green undergrowth, and cool blue sky, creating a scene that feels both wild and intimate. The lively brushwork gives the forest a sense of movement, as if the branches and leaves are shifting in autumn air.

02. The House and the Tree

mom_02_houses_and_trees_after_paul_cezanne.jpg

This study painting by Lin Wang is after Paul Cézanne's "The House and the Tree." The work reinterprets Cézanne's composition of pale houses framed by a dark branching tree, bringing forward the strong architectural shapes and wintery atmosphere of the original. The simplified forms, cool sky, and dark linear branches create a quiet, structured village scene.

As a study after Cézanne, the painting reflects an appreciation for post-impressionist composition and the balance between natural forms and built space. Message @unicagallerynyc for more details about the painting. Follow @unicagallerynyc for more paintings and artist studies.

03. River Town of Zhouzhuang

mom_03_river_town_of_zhouzhuang_after_wu_guanzhong.jpg

This study painting by Lin Wang is after Wu Guanzhong's "River Town of Zhouzhuang" from 1985. The composition presents a quiet water town with white walls, dark rooflines, narrow passages, and softened grey-blue atmosphere. Lin Wang's version keeps the spare structure of the original while translating it into a loose oil-painting language with broader, tactile brushwork.

As a study after Wu Guanzhong, the painting reflects the elegance of Jiangnan architecture and the poetic stillness of a river town. Message @unicagallerynyc for more details about the painting. Follow @unicagallerynyc for more paintings and artist studies.

06. Fiori

mom_06_fiori_after_giorgio_morandi_1952.jpg

This study painting by Lin Wang is after Giorgio Morandi's "FIORI" from 1952. The composition centers on a small arrangement of pale flowers in a white vase, set against a muted, earthy background. The restrained palette and quiet form reflect Morandi's meditative approach to still life, while the heavier texture gives this version a tactile, handmade presence.

08. A View of Auvers-sur-Oise, The Fence

mom_08_a_view_of_auvers_sur_oise_the_fence_after_paul_cezanne.jpg

This study painting by Lin Wang is after Paul Cézanne's "A View of Auvers-sur-Oise, The Fence," c. 1873. The scene looks across a pale foreground fence toward a village nestled into green hills, with trees framing both sides of the composition. The painting follows Cézanne's arrangement while emphasizing lively brushwork and fresh green tonal shifts.

As a study after Cézanne, this work reflects the artist's interest in landscape structure, village forms, and the transition between impressionist light and post-impressionist construction. 09. Vase With Thistles

mom_09_vase_with_thistles_after_vincent_van_gogh_1890.jpg

This study painting by Lin Wang is after Vincent van Gogh's "Flower Vase with Thistles" from 1890. A vivid orange-red vase anchors the composition, while tangled green leaves, blue-purple thistles, and dry golden stems spread outward with dramatic energy. The painting follows Van Gogh's expressive still-life structure while bringing an especially bright, textured surface to the vase and background.

As a study after Van Gogh, the work celebrates movement, color, and the emotional force of a simple flower arrangement. Message @unicagallerynyc for more details about the painting. Follow @unicagallerynyc for more paintings and artist studies.
